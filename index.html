Morsel

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Overview

Morsel is a local-first calorie and macro tracker built for everyday Pakistani and desi food. Morsel does not require an account, and there is no Morsel cloud service. Your nutrition history stays on your device and is not sent to a Morsel server.

This policy explains what information Morsel accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. This policy does not describe "we collect no data" — a couple of features (barcode lookup and advertising) do send limited information off your device, as explained below.

Information Stored on Your Device

Morsel stores the following information locally on your device, using a local database (SQLite). This information is not transmitted to a Morsel server, because no such server exists:

  • Your profile and goals, including an optional name, body metrics, and goal weight
  • Meals you log, custom recipes, weight logs, and activity or workout summaries
  • App preferences and private, on-device product-usage counters
  • Optional normalized health summaries, if you choose to connect Health Connect

Because there is no account and no Morsel cloud database, this data exists only on your device and any backup file you choose to create yourself.

Health Connect (Optional)

Morsel can optionally connect to Android Health Connect. This is permission-gated: Morsel only reads the workout and activity types you specifically approve, which may include exercise sessions, active and total calories burned, distance, steps, and heart rate.

Data read from Health Connect is normalized and stored on your device to power the Progress screen. It is not uploaded anywhere.

You can disconnect Health Connect at any time from Settings within Morsel, and you can revoke Morsel's Health Connect permissions directly in Android system settings.

Barcode Scanning

Morsel uses your device's camera only to scan barcodes on packaged food. When you scan a barcode, that barcode number is sent to the public Open Food Facts API to look up product information.

Your profile information and meal history are never included in this request. Only the scanned barcode is sent.

Advertising

Morsel shows banner ads using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The app is configured to request non-personalized ads.

Even with non-personalized ads, Google may process information such as your device's advertising ID, diagnostic and app interaction data, and an approximate location derived from your IP address or network, in accordance with Google's own policies. Your meal history is never shared with the ads SDK.

For more information, see Google's Ads Policy, Privacy Policy, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Notifications

Morsel can schedule meal reminder notifications. These are scheduled entirely on your device using standard Android notification tools. There is no Morsel push notification server, and no notification data is sent off your device.

Exports and Backups

Your data leaves your device only if you deliberately choose to create and share a backup file, or an optional tester-feedback export.

The tester-feedback export is optional and does not include your profile measurements or meal history.

Data Sharing and Sale

Morsel does not sell your personal information. Morsel does not operate its own analytics server, and Morsel does not share your meal history, profile, or body metrics with any third party. The only data sent off your device is described above: the barcode you scan (sent to Open Food Facts) and standard information processed by Google's advertising services.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

  • You can reset your logged data at any time from Settings within Morsel.
  • You can revoke camera, notification, and Health Connect permissions at any time in Android system settings.
  • Uninstalling Morsel deletes all local app data stored by the app.
  • Because Morsel has no account system and no Morsel cloud database, there is no remote account record to request deletion of.

Children's Privacy

Morsel is not directed at children. It is intended for a general adult audience (18+). Morsel does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Your Morsel data is stored locally on your device. You are responsible for the security of your device and any backup files you choose to create and share.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
